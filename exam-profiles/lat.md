# HEC Law Admission Test (LAT)

**Authority:** HEC Education Testing Council (ETC)  
**Official pattern document:** https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/PublishingImages/LAT%20Final-1.pdf  
**ETC portal:** https://etc.hec.gov.pk/  
**Checked:** 2026-11-24

## Published LAT structure reference

The HEC LAT document describes a 100-mark assessment with 75 MCQs and writing tasks: an essay (15 marks) and personal statement (10 marks). Its MCQ areas include English, General Knowledge, Islamic Studies, Pakistan Studies, Urdu, and Mathematics. The linked document contains the detailed counts and time allocations.

Always confirm the latest LAT notice, schedule, eligibility, and test instructions on ETC. The repository's current bank has partial MCQ-topic overlap but does not yet provide essay/personal-statement marking or a full LAT mock.
