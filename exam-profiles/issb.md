# Inter Services Selection Board (ISSB)

**Authority:** Inter Services Selection Board, Pakistan  
**Official selection system:** https://issb.gov.pk/selection-system  
**Checked:** 2026-11-24

ISSB selection is not a conventional MCQ-only exam. Its official description refers to a flexible selection process using individual and group assessment methods, activities, and sessions to evaluate candidates' suitability and leadership potential. Activities may be adjusted to meet the requirements of the armed forces.

Use official ISSB guidance and the relevant service's recruitment notice. Preparation should focus on communication, teamwork, self-awareness, problem-solving, and fitness where applicable. This repository does not provide personality-test answer keys or claim to predict selection outcomes.
