**Official syllabus page:** https://pmdc.pk/Publication/Syllabus

**Checked:** 2026-11-24

## Current-cycle note

PM&DC's May 19, 2026 public notice stated that the **2025 curriculum/syllabus applied to MDCAT-2026**. This is a historical cycle-specific notice, not a guarantee for MDCAT-2027 or later. Check the current PM&DC syllabus page and notice before studying.

## Preparation areas

Use the official current syllabus for Biology, Chemistry, Physics, English, and Logical Reasoning topic boundaries and learning outcomes. Do not rely on an old academy handout if it conflicts with PM&DC's current syllabus.

## Repository coverage

The question bank contains introductory original practice items in Biology, Chemistry, Physics, English, and reasoning. It is **not yet a complete MDCAT mock bank** and does not claim to reproduce actual MDCAT questions.
