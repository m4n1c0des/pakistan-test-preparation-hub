# FPSC and PPSC recruitment tests

**Authority:** Federal Public Service Commission / Punjab Public Service Commission  
**Official reference:** https://www.fpsc.gov.pk/  
**Checked:** 2026-11-24

## Pattern note
There is no single universal paper pattern for all posts. The advertisement, syllabus, case number, and official instructions for the specific post govern. Some examinations combine MCQs with descriptive papers or other stages.

## Areas to prepare
Post-specific professional knowledge, general ability, English, Pakistan affairs, current affairs, and other listed syllabus areas.

## Candidate checklist
- [ ] Confirm latest advertisement/notice and test date.
- [ ] Confirm eligibility and test category.
- [ ] Download current official syllabus/sample paper, if available.
- [ ] Confirm duration, question types, negative marking, and calculator rules.
- [ ] Build a timed practice plan only after confirming the above.

> This profile is an orientation note, not an official notice or a promise of exact paper composition.
