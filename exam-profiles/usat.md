# HEC Undergraduate Studies Admission Test (USAT)

**Authority:** HEC Education Testing Council (ETC)  
**Official FAQ:** https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/Pages/FAQ%27s.aspx  
**ETC portal:** https://etc.hec.gov.pk/  
**Checked:** 2026-11-24

## Categories and university policy

HEC's ETC FAQ describes six USAT categories and advises candidates to select the category matching their academic stream. HEC has also clarified that USAT is recommended/optional for universities: each university decides whether to accept USAT scores or conduct its own admission test. Do not assume every Pakistani university requires USAT.

Use the current ETC advertisement, category-specific content weightages, and university admission notice. The bank currently supports cross-topic verbal, quantitative, reasoning, and selected subject practice; it is not a complete six-category USAT mock bank.
