## How to prepare

1. Build fundamentals across all ten competency areas; do not study only programming or DSA.
2. Practise code tracing, debugging, scenario analysis, and applied reasoning in addition to definitions.
3. For quantitative/analytical questions, write the reasoning steps and check assumptions.
4. Use the 2026 HEC overview report for historical performance and cognitive-tier context, not as a leaked paper or prediction.
5. Confirm the current cycle's instructions, eligibility, schedule, and sample material on the official NSCT portal.
