# HEC HAT-General / HAAT-Subject

**Authority:** Higher Education Commission Pakistan  
**Official reference:** https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/HECAnnouncements/Pages/Public-Notice.aspx  
**Checked:** 2026-11-24

## Pattern note
HEC has issued public notices about HAT/HAAT arrangements and future admission cycles. Some schedules and operational details are announced separately. Do not assume a proposed or previous schedule is final; re-check HEC announcements and the relevant notice.

## Areas to prepare
HAT-General: verbal, analytical, quantitative. HAAT-Subject: aptitude plus subject/academic/research competencies as described in the relevant HEC document.

## Candidate checklist
- [ ] Confirm latest advertisement/notice and test date.
- [ ] Confirm eligibility and test category.
- [ ] Download current official syllabus/sample paper, if available.
- [ ] Confirm duration, question types, negative marking, and calculator rules.
- [ ] Build a timed practice plan only after confirming the above.

> This profile is an orientation note, not an official notice or a promise of exact paper composition.
