# NTS National Aptitude Test (NAT)

**Authority:** National Testing Service Pakistan  
**Official pattern:** https://www.nts.org.pk/new/nat-paper-pattern.php  
**Checked:** 2026-11-24

## Pattern varies by candidate group

NTS currently lists two broad categories: Category I for 12-year education and Category II for 14-year education. The published table includes six Category-I paper types and five Category-II types. For example:

| Paper type | English | Analytical | Quantitative | Subject | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| NAT-ICS (12-year CS group) | 20 | 20 | 20 | 30 | 90 |
| NAT-IIA (14-year Arts & Humanities) | 20 | 15 | 15 | 50 | 100 |
| NAT-IIB (14-year Biological Sciences) | 10 | 10 | 10 | 70 | 100 |

These examples are not interchangeable. Select the paper type matching your educational background and check the live NTS distribution before preparation.

## Study areas

Verbal/English, analytical reasoning, quantitative reasoning, and the relevant subject component. Subject preparation must match the selected NAT type.
