# NTS GAT General and Subject

**Authority:** National Testing Service Pakistan  
**Official reference:** https://www.nts.org.pk/Products/GATGEN/gat-g-paper-dist.php  
**Checked:** 2026-11-24

## Pattern note
GAT-General category distributions differ by academic field. GAT-Subject has a general component and a subject component. Verify current distribution, eligibility, and validity on NTS pages.

## Areas to prepare
Verbal, quantitative, analytical, and (for GAT-Subject) discipline knowledge.

## Candidate checklist
- [ ] Confirm latest advertisement/notice and test date.
- [ ] Confirm eligibility and test category.
- [ ] Download current official syllabus/sample paper, if available.
- [ ] Confirm duration, question types, negative marking, and calculator rules.
- [ ] Build a timed practice plan only after confirming the above.

> This profile is an orientation note, not an official notice or a promise of exact paper composition.
