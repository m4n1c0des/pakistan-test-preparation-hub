# UET Lahore Combined Entrance Test (ECAT)

**Authority:** University of Engineering and Technology (UET) Lahore  
**Official ECAT information:** https://ecat.uet.edu.pk/General/Ecat  
**Checked:** 2026-11-24

## 2026-cycle pattern reference

The UET ECAT information page for the 2026 cycle described a 100-minute test with 10 English questions and 90 questions distributed across the candidate's selected subject combination. Published combinations included Mathematics/Physics/Chemistry; Mathematics/Physics/Computer Science; Mathematics/Statistics/Computer Science; Biology/Physics/Chemistry; and Mathematics/Statistics/Physics. The page described four marks per correct answer and no negative marking for that cycle.

**Important:** Treat these as 2026-cycle details only. Re-check the official page for the next admission cycle, subject combinations, duration, and marking rules.

## Preparation

Choose the subject combination matching your Intermediate background. Practise conceptual and numerical questions in the relevant subjects, plus English. Do not use one subject combination for every candidate.
