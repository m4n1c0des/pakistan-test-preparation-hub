# Choose the correct test track

1. **Undergraduate admission in Pakistan:** check the target university's admission notice. It may use its own test, USAT, NAT, LAT, MDCAT, ECAT, or another route. Do not assume a national test is mandatory for every institution.
2. **Medical/dental admission:** use the current PM&DC MDCAT syllabus and admission notices.
3. **Engineering admission:** confirm whether ECAT is required and select the correct subject combination for your Intermediate background.
4. **Law:** LAT is for LLB admission; LAW-GAT is a separate assessment for law graduates.
5. **MS/MPhil/PhD:** verify whether the institution requires HAT/GRE/HAAT or another assessment for your programme and intake.
6. **Government job:** download the exact FPSC/PPSC advertisement, case number, and post-specific syllabus.
7. **CSS:** prepare separately for MPT and the descriptive written examination; MCQs alone are not enough.
8. **Defence selection:** ISSB is a multi-method selection process, not a conventional MCQ-only paper.
9. **CS/engineering graduate test:** check the current GATE or university syllabus.
10. **Study abroad:** ask the receiving institution which GRE/IELTS/TOEFL version and score it accepts.
11. **Private-sector job:** review the job description and assessment instructions; practise the relevant coding, SQL, aptitude, or role-based format.

Never prepare from an exam name alone when categories, subject combinations, or post-specific syllabuses exist.
