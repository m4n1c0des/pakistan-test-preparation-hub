# Research notes and source discipline

Exam patterns are not interchangeable. This repository records official information separately from original practice content.

## Current official references checked 2026-11-24

- **NSCT (HEC):** [Areas of Competencies](https://nsct.hec.gov.pk/AreasOfCompetencies) publishes ten competency areas and their weights. [HEC 2026 overview report](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/scholarshipsgrants/Documents/Complete%20NSCT%20Report%20and%20Analytics.pdf) provides historical score/proficiency and cognitive-tier analysis; it is not a future paper prediction.
- **NTS NAT:** [Official NAT pattern](https://www.nts.org.pk/new/nat-paper-pattern.php) distinguishes 12-year and 14-year education categories and paper types.
- **NTS GAT-General:** [Official distribution](https://www.nts.org.pk/Products/GATGEN/gat-g-paper-dist.php) differs by category (A/B/C/D). [GAT-Subject](https://www.nts.org.pk/Products/NTSGATSUB/gat-s-paper-dist.php) has a general and subject component.
- **HEC HAT/HAAT:** [HEC public notice](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/HECAnnouncements/Pages/Public-Notice.aspx) states the Fall 2027 arrangements and says schedules, registration, content weightages and sample papers will be communicated separately. Do not invent those missing details.
- **FPSC/PPSC:** exact advertisement, case number, and post-specific syllabus are authoritative; there is no universal pattern for every post. See [FPSC](https://www.fpsc.gov.pk/) and [PPSC](https://www.ppsc.gop.pk/).
- **GATE CS:** use the year-specific [GATE syllabus](https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/doc/GATE2026_Syllabus/CS_2026_Syllabus.pdf) and information brochure.
- **GRE:** use the current [ETS GRE structure page](https://www.ets.org/gre/test-takers/general-test/prepare/test-structure.html).
- **IELTS:** [Academic](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test) and [General Training](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-general-training-test) share Listening and Speaking, but Reading and Writing differ.
- **TOEFL iBT:** use the current [ETS content page](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/about/content.html?version=after-jan-2026); task formats can change.
- **Technical hiring:** [HackerRank question types](https://support.hackerrank.com/articles/2354192461-question-types-in-hackerrank-tests) shows the breadth of possible assessment formats; employer instructions control the actual test.

## How previous/public papers are handled

Use publicly released official sample papers, syllabi, competency maps, and reports to understand tested skills and style. Do not copy proprietary question banks, paid books, confidential tests, or leaked material. A question in this repository is labelled `original_practice` unless it is a clearly attributed, openly licensed adaptation. A topic tag means alignment, not that the item appeared in an exam.

When updating a profile, record the exact official URL, access/check date, and the specific change. If a detail is not published, say so instead of guessing.

- **MDCAT:** PM&DC's May 19, 2026 notice said the 2025 syllabus applied to MDCAT-2026; do not assume this applies to the next cycle. Check [PM&DC syllabus](https://pmdc.pk/Publication/Syllabus) and the current notice.
- **UET ECAT:** the [UET ECAT page](https://ecat.uet.edu.pk/General/Ecat) describes subject combinations and 2026-cycle format. Re-check future-cycle details.
- **USAT:** [HEC ETC FAQ](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/Pages/FAQ%27s.aspx) describes six categories. HEC has clarified that universities decide whether to accept USAT scores or run their own admission test.
- **LAT:** [HEC LAT pattern document](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/etc/PublishingImages/LAT%20Final-1.pdf) includes MCQs, essay, and personal statement. Verify the current ETC notice.
- **LAW-GAT:** separate from LAT; consult the [ETC portal](https://etc.hec.gov.pk/) and current HEC announcements.
- **CSS:** FPSC maintains separate MPT, written examination, psychological assessment, and viva information under its [CSS section](https://www.fpsc.gov.pk/Jobs?section=CSS).
- **ISSB:** the [official selection-system page](https://issb.gov.pk/selection-system) describes a flexible multi-method assessment process.
