# Question design, provenance, and data policy

## Question quality
- Test one primary learning objective per item.
- Make the stem clear and self-contained.
- Use plausible distractors based on common misconceptions.
- Avoid ambiguous wording, hidden assumptions, and trick wording.
- Include a concise explanation and, for numerical items, show the method.
- Use easy/medium/hard as editorial labels only; pilot-test before claiming calibrated difficulty.
- Review answer keys independently, especially calculations and logic.

## Provenance
- `origin: original_practice` means authored for this repository.
- Do not label an item “past paper” unless its lawful source and exact provenance are documented.
- Do not upload leaked, confidential, or copyrighted test content.
- Link to official sources rather than mirroring protected material.

## Privacy and safety
Do not submit candidate names, roll numbers, phone numbers, private score reports, login details, or other personal information.
